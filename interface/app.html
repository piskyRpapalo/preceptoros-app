Welcome to PreceptorOS

Three steps to begin. Opening this guide does not connect an account, install a model or publish a node.

  1. Discover PreceptorOS. Manage your own AI resources, prepare a node and explore Open World. Your private data stays yours.

  3. Configure your node. Choose a display name and a neutral public-chatbot prompt, then review your node identity. Saving a local draft is not admission to Open World.
